FAMILIA MONDRAGÓN

We are a family passionate about our roots and the tradition of mezcal. Each bottle represents years of history, work, and dedication, passed down from generation to generation.

At Mondragón Family, we believe in authenticity, respect for the land, and sharing unique moments. Our mezcal is not just a drink — it is an experience that connects culture, family, and tradition.

Mission

To offer artisanal mezcal of the highest quality, preserving our traditions and sharing the essence of Mexico with the world.

Vision

To become an internationally recognized brand for our authenticity, quality, and respect for the artisanal process.

Values

Tradition, family, respect for the land, authenticity, and passion for what we do.
