Traditional young mezcal at 45° for those seeking more intense flavor and aroma. Crafted through a double artisanal distillation process, capturing the true essence of agave. It offers a floral and fruity bouquet for a refined tasting experience.

Espadín Mezcal 45% Alc. Vol.

Master Distillers: Fam. Mendez & García

Agave: Agave Angustifolia Haw

Age: 9 to 10 years

Distillation: Double distillation in copper still

Mill: Tahona

Oven: Underground conical pit

Fermentation: Natural in wooden vats

Our Story

Our origins date back to 1795 in Santiago Matatlán, Oaxaca. We are the sixth generation of mezcal producers. We oversee the entire production chain, from cultivation to commercialization, crafting only 100% agave artisanal mezcals from both wild and cultivated agaves. Our mezcal is featured in some of the finest restaurants in Mexico. We honor our traditions by focusing on quality rather than competition. Today, we proudly have over 70 years as a brand.
